# 🎮 About the Game

The city has fallen.

An endless stream of digital ghosts is invading the last secure district, and you're the final operator standing.

Build your defenses, adapt your strategy, and survive increasingly difficult waves as stronger enemies begin to overwhelm your position.

Every tower placement matters.

Every upgrade counts.

Can you protect the protocol?

---

# ⚡ Features

- 🏙️ Fast-paced Tower Defense gameplay
- 👻 Multiple enemy types
- 🔫 Defensive towers with unique roles
- 📈 Progressively harder enemy waves
- 🎵 Original soundtrack
- ✨ Stylized post-processing visuals
- 🌐 Play directly in your browser
